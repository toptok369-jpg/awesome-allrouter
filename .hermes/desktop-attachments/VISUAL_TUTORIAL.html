1 Base Character Concept Generation
In this first stage of the pipeline, we create our base character.

💡 Initial Image Model Flexibility:
The specific initial image generation model you choose does not matter—the priority is simply to generate a high-quality, realistic character portrait. Top recommended options include Krea 2, Nano Banana 2 / Pro, SDXL (with a realistic checkpoint/fine-tune), Z-Image Turbo, etc.

For our primary front portrait, we demonstrate using Krea 2, followed by Nano Banana Pro to generate the matching 180° back-view reference angle.

🎨 Why Nano Banana Pro for Angle Matching?
While we focus on open-source ComfyUI workflows, Nano Banana Pro is a state-of-the-art model that provides the fastest, most practical way to generate matching 180° turned character angles with perfect consistency.

💡 Pro Tips for Krea 2 Photorealism & Best Practices:
  • Camera & Lens Settings: Instead of generic buzzwords like "8k" or "masterpiece", describe your shot using real photography terms like 85mm lens, f/2.0 aperture, and soft studio lighting.
  • Embrace Natural Skin Texture: Include descriptors like subtle skin texture, natural shadows, and candid portrait for authentic realism.
  • Style Reference / Moodboard: Krea 2 excels when provided with a visual style anchor to lock lighting and color tone.
1️⃣ Front View Concept Prompt (Krea 2):
A medium full shot, framed from the mid-thigh up, cowboy shot studio photograph of an attractive young woman with a curvy hour-glass body shape, small waist, wide hips, thick thighs, pale fair skin, bright blue eyes, and long straight black hair, standing and looking directly at the camera. She is wearing a white ribbed crop top with thin spaghetti straps and low-rise denim shorts. Studio lighting, soft shadows, clean neutral grey backdrop, shot on 85mm lens, photorealistic.

1 Reference Motion Video Selection & Facial Consistency Guidelines

To achieve flawless facial consistency and identity retention across your generations in SCAIL-2 (Part 3), pay close attention to how your reference motion video is shot:

💡 Key Motion & Distance Requirement:

Keep the Face Medium-to-Close to the Camera: The subject in your reference video should maintain a clear, medium-to-close distance to the camera lens. If the person moves too far away or turns their face completely out of frame, the AI loses high-resolution facial keypoints, causing the model to hallucinate, shift facial geometry, or alter the character's identity entirely.

🖼️ Character Reference Image Loaders Interface

3 Configure SAM3 Temporal Segmentation Prompts

SAM3 uses targeted text prompts to automatically isolate distinct body parts and clothing items across every frame of the reference video.

🧠 How SAM3 Segmentation Prompts Work:
• head, face, hair: Identifies and tracks the facial region so identity features are seamlessly applied.
• skin, body, arms, legs: Isolates exposed skin regions for natural lighting and texture blending.
• dress, clothes, outfit: Maps clothing boundaries to track motion dynamics and fabric movement.
SAM3 compiles these text prompts into precise 3D temporal tracking vectors, preventing flickering and face-warping across video playback.
🔍 SAM3 Segmentation Prompts Node Interface

4 Configure Motion Generation Text Prompt

Enter your main video motion prompt into the text encoder node.

💡 Prompting Rules & Scene Modifications:
• Keep it Simple & Objective: Write concise descriptions (e.g. "A beautiful woman walking gracefully down a university hallway"). Overly complex prompts can confuse the motion model.
• Targeted Variations: Use this prompt for localized scene or hairstyle changes — for instance, if your base character has short hair but you want long hair flowing dynamically in specific video moments, describe it directly in this prompt!
✍️ Motion Text Prompt Node Interface

5 Execution & Colored Mask Tracking Preview

Upon clicking Queue Prompt (Run), ComfyUI generates real-time colored tracking masks representing the SAM3 temporal segmentation outputs.

🔑 Why Colored Tracking Masks Are Essential in SCAIL-2:
These colored masks are used to select and isolate the person within the scene based on your SAM3 segmentation prompts. They create a precise spatial guidance map for the diffusion UNET, ensuring that character posture, body motion, and clothing remain perfectly tracked throughout the video.

💡 Post-Processing & Mobile Editing Pro-Tip:

To elevate your generated video to cinematic photorealism, it is highly recommended to perform subtle post-processing edits:

  • Add Film Grain / Noise: Adding a touch of subtle noise breaks up digital smoothness and mimics real camera sensors.
  • Lift Shadows: Slightly lowering or adjusting shadow depth softens harsh dark tones and creates natural contrast.

📲 My Personal Workflow: I personally transfer the generated MP4 video directly to my smartphone and use the built-in gallery video editor to quickly apply these noise and shadow adjustments. It takes seconds and yields incredible, organic realism!
